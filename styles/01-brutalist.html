We press what a spreadsheet would reject.

Static Press exists because the bands we love couldn't get a callback from anyone bigger. So we built our own pressing plant relationships, our own mailing list, and our own print run of a zine nobody asked us to make.

Every release is limited on purpose. When it's gone, it's gone — we press again only if the band wants to. That's the whole model.

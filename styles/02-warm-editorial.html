Started in a home oven, four years ago.

Wheat & Ash began as a Sunday hobby — loaves left on neighbors' porches with a note asking what they thought. Three years later we opened a five-table shop two blocks from where it started.

We still bake the same way: slow fermentation, local flour, and small enough batches that we actually taste every loaf before it goes in the case.

"We'd rather sell out by 10am than compromise the bread."

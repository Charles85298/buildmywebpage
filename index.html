Common questions

What to know before getting started.

Clear answers make planning your website easier.

How long does a website take to build?+

Most small-to-medium business website concepts can be created within the first week, with full projects typically completed in roughly four to eight weeks depending on content readiness, feedback, functionality, and scope.

Can you redesign my current website?+

Yes. A redesign can improve appearance, messaging, performance, mobile usability, search readiness, and conversion paths while preserving useful existing content.

Will I be able to update the website myself?+

Yes. Projects can include a content management system and practical training so you can update text, images, articles, products, or other content.

Do you work with clients outside Arizona?+

Yes. Fleming Solutions serves clients nationwide through video meetings, shared project tools, and a clear remote collaboration process.

Do you provide hosting and ongoing support?+

Hosting guidance, maintenance, updates, monitoring, and ongoing improvement plans can be included based on your needs.
